# OAH Threat Model

## Threat Assumptions

OAH assumes:

- At least one independent verification layer exists.
- Evidence preservation channels are not fully compromised.
- Temporal records cannot be rewritten without detection.

## Threat Scenarios

### T1 — Complete Authority Concentration

Single entity controls:

- Decision
- Storage
- Verification

Effect:

External verification collapses.

---

### T2 — Total Collusion

All independent verification layers become coordinated.

Effect:

Verification becomes indistinguishable from narrative.

---

### T3 — Observation Interference

Observation irreversibly alters the decisional state.

Effect:

Reconstruction becomes impossible.

---

## Interpretation

Threat realization does not falsify OAH.

It defines operational risk boundaries.
