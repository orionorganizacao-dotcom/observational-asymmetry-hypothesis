# OAH Structural Flow

```text
Decisional State
        ↓
Observational Asymmetry
        ↓
Need for Independent Preservation
        ↓
Pre-Decisional Commitment
        ↓
Verifiability
        ↓
A-DAP Implementation
```

## Interpretation

A decisional state does not imply access to its own conditions.

Observational Asymmetry emerges when the mechanism producing a decision is also the only mechanism capable of reconstructing it.

This creates the need for independent preservation mechanisms.

Pre-Decisional Commitment introduces temporal evidence before output generation.

Verifiability emerges from preserved evidence rather than retrospective explanation.

A-DAP is an implementation architecture operating under these conditions.
