# Hypotheses

## H1 — Structural Separation

Executing a decision and accessing the internal state responsible for that decision are structurally distinct operations.

## H2 — Reconstruction Constraint

Increasing system complexity increases the difficulty of reconstructing internal decisional states from outputs alone.

## H3 — Observational Asymmetry

There exists an asymmetry between performing a computation and externally observing the state responsible for that computation.

## H4 — Non-Implementation Dependence

Observed asymmetries should persist across different architectures and implementations if OAH is structurally valid.
