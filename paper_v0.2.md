# The Observational Asymmetry Hypothesis

## On the Structural Distinction Between Executing and Accessing a Decisional State

Author:
Ezio v.s. Santos

Affiliation:
Independent Researcher

Version:
v0.2 — STRUCTURAL LOCK

Status:
Foundational Draft

Core proposition:

"Execution does not imply observability."

Observational Asymmetry Hypothesis (OAH):

A system exhibits Observational Asymmetry when no Pre-Decisional Commitment exists for a given output.

In the absence of such commitment, all post-hoc accounts of the decisional state — including those produced by the system itself — constitute reconstruction rather than verification.

Observational Asymmetry establishes a necessary condition for structural unverifiability.

Its absence is necessary but not sufficient for robust verifiability.
