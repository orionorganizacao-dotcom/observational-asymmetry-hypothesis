# EXP003 — Blind Reconstruction Protocol

## Status
Planned

## Objective
Measure whether observers reconstruct causal logic
from outputs without knowing their origin.

## Variables
- Human confidence
- Reconstruction accuracy
- Temporal consistency
- Verification gap

## Procedure

1. Hide source identity
2. Present outputs A/B/C
3. Collect rankings
4. Compare perceived legitimacy vs real origin

## Expected falsifier

No measurable asymmetry between conditions.
